# 🌱 Inspire Prompts

Creative prompts to help you think differently about narrative intelligence and discover new insights.

---

## 🎭 Narrative as Story

### The Plot Thickens
- **If funding flips while mindshare surges, what story is unfolding?**
  - Funding negative (bullish) + mindshare high = Smart money accumulating before price move?
  - Funding positive (bearish) + mindshare high = Retail FOMO before reversal?

- **When velocity accelerates but price sleeps, who's telling the story?**
  - Narrative ahead of price = Early signal or false start?
  - Price ahead of narrative = Momentum without conviction?

- **What happens when smart accounts exit but mentions spike?**
  - Retail entering as smart money exits = Distribution signal?
  - Or new smart accounts replacing old ones = Rotation?

---

### Character Development
- **Imagine: acceleration as a character — what's its role in the plot?**
  - Positive acceleration = Hero rising (momentum building)
  - Negative acceleration = Hero falling (momentum fading)
  - Zero acceleration = Side character (stable, no change)

- **Velocity as the protagonist's journey:**
  - High positive velocity = Hero on quest (strong momentum)
  - Negative velocity = Hero in retreat (narrative fading)
  - Volatile velocity = Hero's internal conflict (uncertainty)

## 🗺️ Mapping Attention

### Draw the Map
- **Where does attention go when price sleeps?**
  - Narrative moves to other tickers = Sector rotation?
  - Narrative stays but velocity drops = Consolidation?
  - Narrative fades = Distribution?

- **When two tickers share accounts, what's the relationship?**
  - High overlap = Same narrative community (correlated)
  - Low overlap = Different communities (independent)
  - Changing overlap = Community migration?

- **Trace the path: Which tickers do smart accounts move between?**
  - BTC → ETH = Sector rotation
  - ETH → SOL = Risk-on rotation
  - Memecoins → Stablecoins = Risk-off rotation

---

### Territory Exploration
- **What narrative territories are unexplored?**
  - Low mindshare tickers with high velocity = Emerging narratives?
  - High mindshare tickers with low velocity = Mature narratives?

- **Where are the narrative borders?**
  - Account overlap boundaries = Sector definitions
  - Velocity correlation clusters = Market structure
  - Mindshare gaps = Narrative voids

## 🔮 Pattern Recognition

### Temporal Patterns
- **What patterns emerge when you layer velocity over acceleration?**
  - Velocity up + Acceleration up = Exponential growth
  - Velocity up + Acceleration down = Growth slowing
  - Velocity down + Acceleration down = Exponential decay

- **How do anomalies relate to mean reversion?**
  - Positive anomaly (spike) = Continuation or mean reversion?
  - Negative anomaly (drop) = Reversal or consolidation?

---

### Cross-Signal Patterns
- **When narrative and price diverge, what's the resolution?**
  - Narrative catches up to price = Confirmation
  - Price catches up to narrative = Momentum
  - Both reverse = False signal

- **What happens when all signals align?**
  - Narrative + Market + On-chain bullish = High confidence
  - Narrative + Market + On-chain bearish = High confidence
  - Mixed signals = Low confidence, wait for clarity

## 🎨 Creative Combinations

### The Fusion Experiment
- **Combine narrative velocity with funding rate:**

- **Layer account churn with price action:**

- **Can you trade narrative without price?**

  - Narrative + Price = Multi-factor approach
